The bracket model, tested for real

The model prices every team's title chance from the bracket. When its number differs from the market by more than the fee, that gap is the "edge". This is what happened when we ran it on Kalshi prices from before the tournament, then settled every trade.

!

Method and caveats

What was tested

  • 2026 Crown: 8 teams. Champion: West Virginia.
  • 2026 NCAA men: 64 teams. Champion: Michigan.
  • Prices: Kalshi historical 1-minute candles, midpoint of YES bid/ask.
  • Ratings: Elo from regular-season results before the tournament; no future games.
  • Model: exact bracket simulation, 1000 jittered runs, 2.0c fee.

What it does not prove

  • 2 tournaments is a tiny sample. One champion is close to a coin flip.
  • The +11.4c average is driven by one 8c winner; the error bar is ±17.3c.
  • Midpoints are not fills. An 8c contract may not absorb $100.00 of cash.
  • No slippage, no liquidity limit, no taxes, no correlation adjustment.
  • The model is a gap finder, not a better forecaster: the market beat it on the largest tournament.
